info 1. Informational Purposes Only

The safety ratings, neighborhood indexes, lighting indicators, transit maps, and infrastructure density scores published on Know Your City (knowyourcitys.in) are provided strictly for general informational, educational, and relocation guidance purposes.

While we make every effort to ensure the accuracy and timeliness of our computational algorithms and datasets, we cannot and do not guarantee 100% real-time accuracy or absolute safety in any neighborhood.

map 2. Open Data & Third-Party Source Attribution

Our algorithms synthesize geographic nodes from trusted third-party open datasets:

  • OpenStreetMap (OSM): Licensed under the Open Data Commons Open Database License (ODbL). Node locations for police stations, clinics, street lighting, and transit hubs are community-mapped.
  • Wikipedia API: City demographic, administrative, and cultural overviews are provided under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike License.

Know Your City is not responsible for omissions, temporary closures, or errors present in underlying open-source geographic datasets.
