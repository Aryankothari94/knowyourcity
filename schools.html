School Infrastructure & Child Safety Evaluation Guide

For relocating families with children, proximity to reputed CBSE, ICSE, and international curriculum schools is a paramount consideration. Evaluating the neighborhood traffic calm zones, pedestrian school crossings, and dedicated school bus corridors is essential.

🎒 Safe School Commute Radius

An optimal residential location minimizes daily school bus commute time to under 25 minutes, reducing transit fatigue and maximizing study/play balance.

🚸 Pedestrian Crossing & Traffic Calming

Check whether school access roads feature speed breakers, dedicated zebra crossings, and morning/afternoon traffic wardens to ensure safe pickup and drop-off.

🛡️ School Security Verification

Look for perimeter fencing, CCTV surveillance at entry gates, background-verified transport drivers, and GPS-enabled school bus tracking apps.
