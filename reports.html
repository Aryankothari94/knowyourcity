The Know Your City Safety Index (KYC-SI) evaluates neighborhood liveability by measuring spatial proximity to essential civic nodes. Each factor is scored on a normalized 0-10 scale and weighted according to urban planning best practices:

Pillar / Dimension Primary Geodata Source Effective Weight Key Metric Measured
Emergency Infrastructure OpenStreetMap / Police Registries 35% Distance to operational Police Station & 24/7 Trauma Hospital (< 2.5km)
Lighting & Transit Accessibility Municipal GIS / OSM Transit Nodes 25% Streetlight node density & distance to active Metro/Bus Terminal (< 800m)
Civic & Commercial Vitality OpenStreetMap POIs 20% Presence of pharmacies, grocery stores, schools, and family parks
Community Verification KYC Verified Local Resident Reviews 20% Evening walk safety ratings, hazard reports, and incident alerts
