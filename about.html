lightbulb Why We Built Know Your City

Every year, millions of Indians relocate to new cities for higher education, career growth, or better livelihoods. Yet, the most fundamental question remains difficult to answer: "Is this neighborhood safe for me and my family?"

Traditional real estate platforms emphasize property prices and superficial amenities while obscuring crucial living factors: distance to the nearest 24/7 police station, street lighting coverage after 10 PM, hospital proximity, and reliable public transit routes. Know Your City was founded to bridge this informational divide by providing transparent, data-backed neighborhood intelligence.

query_stats Our Data Methodology & Algorithmic Rigor

Our safety scoring engine aggregates geospatial nodes across multiple verifiable open datasets and community feedback channels:

1. Emergency Response Density (35%)

Calculates spatial distance and walking/driving travel time to operational police stations, fire stations, and 24/7 hospitals.

2. Street Illumination & Transit (25%)

Surveys street lighting density, active metro stations, well-lit bus terminals, and late-night transport corridors.

3. Community Vitality & Density (20%)

Examines commercial foot traffic, open markets, schools, family parks, and residential occupancy ratios.

4. Verified Resident Feedback (20%)

Incorporates localized ratings, safety incident alerts, and neighborhood tips submitted by authenticated local residents.
