Cookie & Tracking Policy

Last updated: September 5, 2026

help_center 1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile) when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work properly, provide personalized functionality, remember your preferences, and deliver relevant advertisements.

At Know Your City (knowyourcitys.in), we use cookies and browser local storage to provide you with seamless city navigation, safe route finding, and localized neighborhood insights.

category 2. Categories of Cookies We Use

Cookie Type Purpose Lifespan
Strictly Necessary User authentication session, security tokens, login state, and CSRF protection. Session / 30 Days
Functional & Preferences Saves selected city (e.g., Solapur, Mumbai), filter choices, map zoom level, and dark theme settings. 1 Year
Analytics & Performance Aggregates anonymous metrics on feature usage, popular neighborhoods, and page speed diagnostics. Up to 2 Years
Google AdSense & Advertising Serves contextual and personalized ads via Google network, limits ad repetition, and measures ad performance. Up to 13 Months

ads_click 3. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising Cookies

We partner with Google AdSense to display advertisements across our website. Google uses cookies, including the DoubleClick DART cookie, to serve relevant advertisements to users based on their prior visits to Know Your City and other internet sites.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to your sites and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising at any time by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can also opt out of third-party vendor advertising cookies by visiting aboutads.info/choices.

tune 4. How to Manage and Disable Cookies

You have full control over cookies and can adjust your browser settings to refuse cookies or alert you when cookies are being sent:

  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and Security → Third-party cookies.
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data.
  • Apple Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Block all cookies.
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Manage and delete cookies.

Note: Disabling strictly necessary cookies may affect your ability to log in or save personalized city safety alerts.
